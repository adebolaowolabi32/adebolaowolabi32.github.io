CSV Kit

Read and validate email CSV files one row at a time.

I extracted this reader from Fieldnotes Academy so other PHP apps can use it. Row numbers let the calling application save its place and resume an import.

Published on Packagist. Framework-independent PHP. Tested on PHP 8.3, 8.4 and 8.5.
